    <b>FAQS</b>

    <p><em>How much does a website cost?</em><br />
    Pricing depends on the size and scope of the site. Most small-business projects start with a deposit, with the remaining balance due before launch. I’ll give you a clear estimate before any work begins.</p>

    <p><em>What is included in the base website?</em><br />
    The base site includes design and development for a straightforward small-business website, mobile-friendly page layouts, basic service and contact information, and launch assistance.</p>

    <p><em>How much time will you need from me?</em><br />
    Usually less than people expect. I’ll need basic business information, any existing logo or photos, examples of sites you like if you have them, and timely feedback during review.</p>

    <p><em>Can you update an existing website?</em><br />
    Yes. I can update an existing site if the current setup allows it and the needed changes are clear. If rebuilding would be simpler or more cost-effective, I’ll say so.</p>

    <p><em>What if I don’t like something in the first version?</em><br />
    That is part of the process. The first version gives us something concrete to review, adjust, and improve before launch.</p>

    <p><em>Do you help with hosting and domain choices?</em><br />
    Yes. I can help you understand the basic options for hosting, domains, and launch setup so you know what you’re paying for and what needs to be maintained.</p>

    <p><em>Do you offer ongoing maintenance?</em><br />
    Ongoing maintenance is not included in the base project, but it can be arranged separately if you want help with updates after launch.</p>

    <p><em>Can I pay for a consultation before deciding on a full site?</em><br />
    Yes. A paid website review or consultation can help clarify what you need before committing to a larger project.</p>

    <p><em>What kind of websites do you build?</em><br />
    I build practical small-business websites that explain what you do, show customers how to contact you, and present your business clearly online.</p>
