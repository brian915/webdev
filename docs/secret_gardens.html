Is your business hiding in a secret garden?

A Facebook page can feel like a lovely walled garden — but people can only find you inside it if they already follow you and have the key.

  • When people need a service, they search Google or ask an AI assistant — they don't scroll a feed hoping to stumble across you.
  • Plenty of your customers don't use Facebook at all, or avoid it on purpose.
  • If your business only lives on Facebook, most of those people never see you.

A Facebook-only presence keeps you hidden

Searchers can't find you

When someone searches for a local service — or asks an AI assistant to recommend one — a Facebook page barely turns up. You're invisible to everyone who isn't already following you.

Not everyone has a key

Many people don't use Facebook, or steer clear of it. When your hours, services, and contact details sit behind a login, you've quietly told those customers they can't do business with you.

You're renting the garden

Facebook owns the walls. A change to their rules, a drop in who sees your posts, or a locked account can take your audience away overnight — and there's nothing you can do about it.

Your own website opens the gate

  • Get found where people search: A real website is something Google and AI assistants can read and recommend, so you show up when customers are actually looking for what you do.
  • Look like a real business: An independent site signals an established, trustworthy business — not a side project. When someone's choosing between you and a competitor, that tips the decision your way.
  • Own your storefront: Your site, your web address, your customers. No algorithm, ad, or account change can take it away from you.
